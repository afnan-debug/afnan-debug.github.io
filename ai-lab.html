Crypto laundering patterns the model looks for

Defensive red flags investigators use. These are patterns to detect, not instructions.

Mixer exposure

Funds pass through services built to blur their origin.

Check: share of value touching mixing services.

Chain-hopping

Value is swapped across blockchains to break the trail.

Check: number of cross-chain swaps in a short window.

Peel chains

A large balance is shaved into many small transfers.

Check: repeated small splits with one large remainder.

Fresh mule wallets

New wallets receive and forward funds within hours.

Check: wallet age and time between inflow and outflow.

Cyber-enabled cash-out

Account takeover, SIM swaps, or stolen API keys end in a withdrawal.

Check: security events just before the transfer.

Sanctions exposure

Direct or indirect links to listed entities.

Check: hops to a flagged counterparty.

Portfolio demonstration only. All cases, wallets, and weights are synthetic and hand-set for teaching. The model is not trained on real data and does not make legal, regulatory, or automated financial-crime determinations. A human investigator makes the final decision.
